Questions & answers

What does RoadGuard GH do?

It records the real speed, the time, the place and the speed limit on every trip, and warns you by voice before you go over the limit. It works for drivers, for passengers who want to see how fast their driver is going, and for families who want to follow a loved one’s trip live.

Where does the speed limit come from?

From your GPS position and OpenStreetMap, the free public map. If a road has a posted limit on the map, RoadGuard uses it. If not, it uses Ghana's general rules: 30 near schools, markets and hospitals, 50 on local and neighbourhood roads and on main roads in towns, 90 on main roads outside towns, and 100 on motorways. You can't type in your own limit, so the record stays trustworthy.

The limit on a sign is different from the app. Why?

RoadGuard has no camera, so it can't read signs. Many Ghanaian roads aren't on the map with their limits yet, so on those roads the app uses the general rules above. When a sign's limit is added to OpenStreetMap, RoadGuard picks it up automatically within about 30 days.

Why is my car's speedometer a little higher than RoadGuard?

Car speedometers are built to read slightly high, by law. GPS shows your true speed. Use Settings → Match my car's speedometer so the screen matches your dial. Your saved records always keep the true GPS speed.

Does it record with the screen off?

In the Android app, yes: start a journey, then you can lock the phone. Allow Location: all the time and Notifications when asked. On the website, keep the screen on, because a web page can't record while the phone is locked.

Can RoadGuard start by itself when I drive?

Yes. In Settings → Auto-start: Start by itself when I’m moving begins recording when RoadGuard is open and you go faster than 15 km/h. In the Android app, Ask me when I start driving sends a notification even when RoadGuard is closed; tap it to start. You can also choose your car’s Bluetooth, so RoadGuard reacts whenever your phone connects to your car. Android doesn’t let any app start recording your location from fully closed without you knowing, so that last step is one tap. Journeys that start by themselves can also end by themselves after 8 minutes parked.

I’m a passenger. How do I use RoadGuard?

Open RoadGuard and tap Start Journey when you get into the trotro, taxi, Bolt or bus. You’ll see how fast the vehicle is going and the speed limit. If the driver goes too fast, you’ll know. Please don’t argue with a driver while the vehicle is moving: stay safe, and report it later if you need to.

What does the voice tell me?

Each time you enter a new speed zone, RoadGuard tells you where you are and the new limit, for example: “You’ve reached Madina, on the Legon Road. The speed limit here is 50 kilometres per hour.” It names the town, and the main road when it knows it. It also warns you when a lower limit is coming up, tells you when you go over, and thanks you when you slow back down. You can turn the voice off or pick another voice in Settings.

Is the demo drive real?

No. The demo is a pretend trip from Accra to Tema so you can see and hear how RoadGuard works. It doesn’t use your location, and a yellow DEMO box on the screen shows what the voice is saying. The trip report at the end is marked as a demo and can’t be used as evidence.

How does Share my trip work?

During a journey, tap Share my trip live. Choose the type of vehicle, type the number plate and where you’re going (all optional), then send the link on WhatsApp. Your family opens the link on any phone, with no app needed, and sees your speed, the speed limit, every town you pass and when you arrive. Sharing stops when you end the journey or tap Stop sharing. You need to be signed in to share. The free plan includes 5 shared trips a month; Plus is unlimited. An SOS always works, even after your 5 shares.

Can my family get alerts?

Yes. On the live trip page, they tap Alert me if they speed, arrive or need help. Their phone will beep, vibrate and show a notification if the vehicle goes over the limit, when you arrive, or if you send an SOS.

What does the SOS button do?

The red SOS button on the Drive screen sends “SOS: I need help” with your location and live link to family on WhatsApp. It also has one-tap calls to 112 (emergency), 191 (police) and 193 (ambulance).

What is the night check-in?

When you’re sharing a trip between 8 pm and 5 am and the vehicle stops for more than 10 minutes, RoadGuard asks “Are you okay?”. Tap I’m fine and your family sees it. If you don’t answer within 2 minutes, your family is told so they can call you.

What is the trip safety score?

After every trip you get a grade from A (excellent) to E (dangerous), based on how much of the trip was within the limit and how often and how far the vehicle went over. Tap Share my score to send a picture of it to WhatsApp Status.

How do I report a problem or a wrong speed limit?

Go to Settings → Contact us, choose what it’s about (for example Wrong speed limit on a road), type your message and tap Send message. You can also email roadguardgh@gmail.com.

What is the red dot on my profile picture?

It means something needs your attention, for example your payment was confirmed or your free trial ends soon. Tap your profile picture to see it. Allow notifications when asked, so these also pop up on your phone.

I have an iPhone. Can I use RoadGuard?

Yes. Open roadguard-gh.web.app in Safari and use it there. Keep the screen on while you drive, because a web page can’t record with the phone locked. The App Store app is coming soon.

Does it use a lot of data or battery?

Data is light: it downloads small map pieces for the roads you drive and saves them for 30 days. GPS does use battery, so on long trips keep the phone on a charger.

I got a speeding SMS. What should I do?

Open the Ticket tab and paste the SMS. RoadGuard checks it for signs of a scam (real notices come from GPS-MTTD, contain no links, and payment is only through ghana.gov.gh or *222#). It then compares the time and place with your journeys and can prepare a contest letter. You have 14 days to pay or contest.

Is this proof the police must accept?

No app can promise that. RoadGuard gives you a detailed record of your speed, time and place, with a check that shows whether it was changed afterwards. That record supports your side when you contest, but the decision rests with the authorities.

Who can see my journeys?

Your journeys are stored on your phone only. They leave your phone only if you join a vehicle in Fleet (the owner sees trips in their vehicle), or if you share a trip live (people with the link can follow it). If you make an account, your name and email are stored so you can sign in. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How does Fleet work?

An owner adds a vehicle and gets a join code. Each driver enters the code in Fleet. The owner then sees every trip in that vehicle, who was driving, and how fast.

How much does it cost?

Downloading RoadGuard is free, and every new user gets 30 days of everything. After that, the core stays free forever. You only pay for the extras you want: Plus from GH₵20 a month, Family from GH₵35 a month, or Fleet at GH₵40 per vehicle a month. Longer plans cost less per month.

What is free and what is in Plus?

Always free: live speed, automatic speed limits, voice warnings, SOS, night check-ins, the trip safety score, 5 shared trips a month, your last 7 days of journeys, and the scam check on any speeding SMS.
Plus adds: unlimited Share my trip with arrival time and family alerts, every journey kept, full reports and the evidence file, and comparing a ticket with your trip plus the contest letter.

How do I pay?

Tap your profile picture, then See plans. Choose a plan and tap Pay with Paystack. You can pay with MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo Mobile Money, or with a bank card. Paystack is a secure payment company; RoadGuard never sees your PIN or card number. Your plan is switched on as soon as we confirm the payment, usually within a few hours. You can also send MoMo directly and enter the transaction ID from the SMS.

What is the Family plan?

Family gives Plus to you and up to 3 more people. After paying, open Account → My family and add the email address each person signs in to RoadGuard with. They get Plus straight away, for as long as your plan is active. It’s ideal for parents following school runs.

How does Fleet pricing work?

Fleet is for anyone who owns vehicles driven by others: trotro, taxi, ride-hailing or company cars. It costs GH₵40 per vehicle a month (less on longer plans) and includes Plus for the owner. You see every trip in each vehicle: who drove, where and how fast. Drivers join with your code for free.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Nothing stops working that keeps you safe. Speed limits, voice warnings, SOS and the safety score stay free. Journeys older than 7 days, reports, ticket contests and unlimited sharing show a lock, with a button to upgrade. Your journeys are never deleted: upgrade and they unlock again.

How do I install or update the Android app?

Open roadguard-gh.web.app in the Chrome app and tap Get the free Android app, then Open → Install (or Update). If the download stops at 100%, you probably opened the link from WhatsApp or a QR scanner, so open it in Chrome itself.

How do I sign in?

Tap Continue with Google for one-tap sign-in, on the app or the website. You can also use an email and password. If you forget your password, tap Forgot password?. Both ways open the same account.

The voice is hard to hear. What can I do?

In Settings, try another Voice and set Speaking speed to Slow. Turn up the phone's media volume, or connect to the car by Bluetooth. Updating Speech Services by Google in the Play Store adds clearer voices.

About this test version

ROADGUARD GH field test 1.2.5. Journeys are stored only on this phone. Speed limits are set automatically from your GPS position using OpenStreetMap: a posted limit where one is mapped, otherwise an estimate from Ghana's general limits (30 near schools, markets and hospitals; 50 on any local or neighbourhood road without a sign, and on main roads in built-up areas; 90 on main roads outside towns; 100 on motorways). Map data for roads you drive is saved on the phone, so repeat routes work even with weak internet. Limits can't be edited by hand.

RoadGuard records your speed with your phone's GPS. It is not a calibrated police instrument, and its reports support a contest rather than prove one.
